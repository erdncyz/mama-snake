Mamba Snake is a fast territory-capture arcade game: swipe to move, close your trail, and claim the board — without getting caught by the snake.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe in four directions to leave the safe zone
• Reconnect your trail to claim new territory
• Reach 75% claimed area to clear the level
• Don’t touch the snake — or your unfinished trail

MAKE IT YOURS
• Play as spider, scarab, ladybug, bee, butterfly, or scorpion
• Pick rival snake colors with animated personality
• Switch arenas: Space, Neon, Jungle, Ocean, Volcano, Desert Night
• Customize menu themes and toggle the arena grid

KEEP PUSHING
• Rising speed and tighter challenges every level
• Earn +1 life every 10 levels
• Watch an ad to revive and keep your run going
• Set a nickname and compete on the online leaderboard

Designed for iPhone and iPad. Simple to learn, hard to put down.
